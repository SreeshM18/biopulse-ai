## 🏆 The 4 Role-Based Access Portals

BioPulse AI features 4 distinct, cryptographically partitioned access portals:

1. **👤 1. Patient Portal (Sovereign Health):**
   * Continuous wearable and vitals telemetry ($HR, SpO_2, BP, Temp$).
   * Prescription vault with adherence tracking and refill countdowns.
   * Access consent logs with 1-hour self-destructing doctor access passes.
   * Universal Emergency Health Passport management.

2. **🚨 2. Emergency Access (NOVA Rescue):**
   * **2-Second Scannable QR Triage:** Instant display of Blood Group, Critical Allergies, and Active Medications.
   * **Universal SOS Dispatch:** Real-time GPS capture with 13 emergency types (Cardiac, Stroke, Trauma, Burns, Bleeding, etc.).
   * **Smart Ambulance Finder:** Automatic dispatch of ALS, Cardiac, ICU, or NICU transport units with live 8-stage route tracking.
   * **Break-Glass Override:** Audited 1-hour emergency clinical unlock for unconscious/incapacitated patients.

3. **👨‍⚕️ 3. Doctor Portal (Clinical Command & Whole-Body Hub):**
   * **Whole-Body Organ Matrix:** 5-organ telemetry (Brain/GCS, Heart/MAP, Lungs/PaO2:FiO2, Blood/Renal Lactate, Liver/Metabolism).
   * **Dynamic Specialty Switcher:** 33 medical specialties with 1-click clinical stat order bundles.
   * **TreeSHAP Explainable Risk:** Mathematical breakdown of physiological drivers ($SpO_2 \downarrow +35\%, RR \uparrow +26\%$).
   * **Automated SOAP Notes:** 1-Click generated clinical consultation notes with PDF export.

4. **🏥 4. Hospital & Labs Portal (Care Units & Diagnostics):**
   * **21 Hospital Departments Grid:** Real-time capacity meters, HOD leadership, and on-call specialist rosters.
   * **Diagnostic PACS & Reports:** Chest X-Rays ($94.8\%$ AI ARDS Match), Brain MRIs, and Arterial Blood Gas panels.
   * **Nurse Station & eMAR:** Bedside barcode verification with `Pending`, `Given`, `Delayed`, `Missed` status tracking.
   * **Outpatient Appointments:** Status tracking (`Scheduled`, `Checked In`, `Completed`) with WebRTC teleconsult launch.
